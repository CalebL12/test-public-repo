The offset was built before the gain ever arrived.

Tax-loss harvesting for a liquidity event

← Who we serve

The situation

A company buyout was on the horizon, and with it more than $10 million in capital gain exposure that would land in a single tax year.

What we did together

Rather than wait for the gain to arrive, we put a long-short investment implementation to work in advance, systematically harvesting losses so the offset was already built when the buyout closed.

Where it landed

Over $10 million in capital gain exposure offset, in place before the transaction rather than after it.
