Tariff Trepidation

April 3, 2025 · Chris Winkler

← All perspectives

When market turbulence hits—especially when it’s triggered by high-profile policy shifts like Trump’s tariffs—the natural impulse can be to step back, panic, and respond reactively. However, history and experience teach us that maintaining composure and staying invested can often be the smarter, more rewarding approach.

Embracing Market Volatility

Volatility isn’t a sign that our investment portfolios are broken; it’s simply the market reflecting the uncertainties of the day. Tariffs, while disruptive, are just one chapter in the broader economic narrative. Markets have always reacted to political events, and though these reactions may seem dramatic in the short run, they don’t upend the long-term upward trajectory of a diversified investment portfolio. In moments like these, the wisdom lies in remembering that markets are built to absorb shocks and evolve.

The Advantage of Composure

Maintaining composure in volatile times does more than just protect your financial assets— it also preserves your decision-making clarity. Emotional decisions can lead to selling at the worst possible times, locking in losses that might have been temporary. By staying calm, you give yourself the space to analyze the fundamentals of your investments rather than getting swept up in the noise of the day. A measured approach often reveals that the underlying strengths of companies and markets remain intact, even if prices temporarily falter.

Global Diversification and Fixed Income: A Positive Buoy

In 2025, as the US market experienced a notable pullback amid heightened uncertainty over policy shifts, many investors found solace in global markets and fixed income securities. This period underscored the timeless value of diversification. While US equities were grappling with short-term headwinds, international markets—benefiting from differing economic cycles and regional growth dynamics—provided a complementary source of returns.

Fixed income instruments, long regarded as the cornerstone of a resilient portfolio, played an equally critical role. Bonds and similar securities offered a degree of stability, cushioning portfolios against the volatility witnessed in the US market. This dual strategy of blending global exposure with fixed income not only mitigated risk but also capitalized on opportunities that arose outside the domestic arena, reaffirming the importance of a well-rounded, evidence-based investment approach.

By incorporating these global and fixed income elements, investors were better positioned to weather the storm, transforming what could have been a period of loss into one of strategic strength and opportunity.

Volatility Into Opportunity

Volatility isn’t the enemy—it’s an invitation. In our experience, market turbulence creates opportunities to acquire quality assets at attractive prices, provided we remain patient and methodical. Short-term shocks often distract from the underlying strength of a business or a market, and by staying the course, we harness these fluctuations to our advantage. When uncertainty grips the market, a disciplined, evidence-based strategy allows us to buy into resilience and future growth rather than sell in fear. It’s about turning every dip into a chance to position ourselves for long-term success, where measured risk and careful analysis prevail over emotional decision-making.

A Perspective Rooted in Evidence-Based Investing

At its core, investing is less about reacting to every headline and more about understanding the broader picture. Trump’s tariffs might generate short-term uncertainty, but for those of us who subscribe to evidence-based strategies, such events are simply a part of the larger economic cycle. A diversified portfolio—carefully constructed based on rigorous research—has the built-in resilience to withstand these shocks. Over time, the market tends to reward discipline and patience, reaffirming the wisdom of staying the course.

Final Thoughts

The chapters of market history are filled with moments of upheaval, yet those who remain invested often find that these periods offer opportunities rather than setbacks. By staying true to a long-term, evidence-based approach, we harness the market’s natural ability to recover and grow, turning temporary volatility into a steppingstone for future success.

Staying calm, staying invested, and keeping sight of the long-term picture are not just investment strategies—they are the hallmarks of a disciplined and thoughtful approach to navigating our ever-changing world.
